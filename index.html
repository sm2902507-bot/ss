        ❓ Help & Support

        Everything you need to get the most from SerenityBot.

        🔍
        💬 How do I start a conversation?

        Just type how you're feeling in the chat box and press Send — or tap one of the quick-reply chips like "I feel anxious". SerenityBot will respond warmly and guide you from there.

        🎤 How does voice chat work?

        Tap the microphone 🎤 button in the chat header and speak naturally. Your words will appear in the text box and send automatically. The speaker 🔊 button reads the last bot response aloud. Voice works in Chrome, Edge, and Safari 16+.

        🌐 How do I change the language?

        Click the language picker button (shows flag + language name) in the top-right on the login screen, or at the bottom of the sidebar inside the app. We support 10 languages including English, Tamil, Hindi, Spanish, French, Arabic, German, Japanese, Portuguese, and Chinese.

        🌬️ How does the breathing exercise work?

        Go to the Breathing tab and choose a technique — 4-7-8 (deep relaxation), Box Breathing (focus), or Simple 4-4 (quick calm). Press Start and follow the expanding/contracting circle. It runs for 5 cycles and stops automatically.

        📊 What is the Mood Log?

        The Mood Log lets you record how you feel each day with a simple emoji (from 😄 Great to 😰 Stressed) plus an optional note. Your entries are saved privately on your device. Checking in regularly helps you notice patterns over time.

        ✨ What are Affirmations?

        Affirmations are short, positive statements that help shift your mindset. Tap Next Affirmation to see a new one, or browse the grid below. You can copy any affirmation to your clipboard with the Copy button.

        🌸 Why do flowers bloom when I move my cursor?

        SerenityBot uses your profile to create a personalised cursor effect — flowers bloom for women, rockets and stars for men, balloons for children, butterflies for others. It's a small reminder that you bring beauty wherever you go. You can turn this off in Settings.

        🔒 Is my data safe and private?

        Yes — completely. Your password is hashed with SHA-256 and a unique salt before storage. Your chat history, mood logs, and profile are stored only on your own device using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server.

        🗑 How do I clear my chat history?

        Click the 🗑 Clear button in the top-right of the chat view. This removes all messages from the current session. You can also clear all stored data from Settings → Clear All My Data.

        📱 Can I use this on my phone?

        Yes! SerenityBot is fully mobile-responsive. Open it in your phone's browser and tap Share → Add to Home Screen to install it like an app. The bottom navigation bar and sidebar drawer work with touch.

        🚨 What if I need urgent help?

        SerenityBot is a supportive companion — not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis or need urgent support, please reach out to a trusted person or a mental health helpline in your country. You are not alone, and help is always available. 💙

        Our Purpose

        SerenityBot was created to give everyone — regardless of age, gender, or background — a warm, private space to process emotions, find calm, and feel supported. We believe mental wellbeing should be accessible to all.

        🛡️

        Your Privacy

        Everything stays on your device. Your passwords are hashed with SHA-256 and a unique random salt. No data is sent to any server, ever. No accounts are tracked. No analytics. Just you and your safe space.

        🌍

        Built for Everyone

        SerenityBot supports 10 languages with full voice input and output. It adapts its tone, colours, and cursor effects based on your age, gender, and role — so it always feels like it was made just for you.

        🧠

        How It Works

        The chatbot uses keyword-based intent matching with a large bank of caring, tailored responses. It recognises anxiety, stress, sadness, loneliness, sleep issues, relationship challenges, and more — responding with empathy and practical tools.

        🌸

        A Note on Safety

        SerenityBot is a supportive companion, not a medical or clinical tool. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please seek professional support. You deserve real care, and help is always available. 💜
